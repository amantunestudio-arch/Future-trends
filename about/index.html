About us

About Future Trends

Future Trends is a small publication that explains where everyday technology is heading, without hype and without jargon.

What we do

We pick one area of daily life, such as housing, food or dining, and look at the changes that are already starting. Every article shows a few concrete examples so you can picture how the change would feel in real life.

How we keep it honest

Each piece separates what is already in use from what is still being tested. We mention costs, limits and local rules where they matter, because a good idea is not always an easy one to adopt.

Who it is for

Curious readers, students, small business owners and anyone who wants a quick, reliable picture of what may be around the corner. If you would like to suggest a topic, use the contact page.

Quick answers

The questions people ask most.

Who writes Future Trends?

The articles are written by the Future Trends editorial team, who research each topic and review it before it goes live.

Do you sell anything?

No. The site exists to explain ideas. We do not promote specific products or companies.

Can I suggest a topic?

Yes. Send it through the contact page and we will consider it for a future article.
